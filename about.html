Our story

A studio in the Blue City.

Komaura began with a single thought: that a nail set could be a small ritual — a quiet, private kind of luxury.

We started in 2023 with a folding table, a few brushes, and a window that looked out over Jodhpur's blue rooftops. Two years later, we are still hand-painting every set in that same room.

Komaura sets are made of long-wear soft gel — flexible, comfortable, and reusable up to three weeks. Each tip is hand-shaped, hand-painted, and quality-checked twice before it leaves our studio.

Slow craft

We make small batches. We wait for the paint to cure properly. We never rush a chrome finish. The result is a set that feels heavier, softer, and more considered than anything mass-produced.

For the woman who notices

Komaura is for the woman who chooses things slowly — her perfume, her fabric, her morning light. We make for her.

— the Komaura studio
